An optional intro paragraph sits above the cards. Each top-level list item is one card; a leading `**bold**` line is the card title.

- **Text card**
  > A quote inside a card renders as its body.
- **Image card**
  ![Icon](ref-square.svg)
  Images that lead a card render as a small icon row; several in one paragraph sit side by side.
- **Nested list**
  - one
  - two
  - three
- **Four cards** fit in one row; five or more wrap to a grid.
