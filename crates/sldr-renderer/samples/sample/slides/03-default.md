# Three things to notice

This is the everyday slide. A title, a short paragraph that sets context, and a few points that carry the argument.

- **Hierarchy.** Headings, body, and emphasis should feel related but distinct.
- **Rhythm.** Bullets sit on a consistent vertical baseline; nothing crowds the edge.
- **Restraint.** Color is used to mean something — accents, not decoration.

If this slide looks balanced at a glance, the flavor is doing its job.
